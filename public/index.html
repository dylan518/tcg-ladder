How to play
  • You and a partner each control a key on a 3×3 board. In each trial one of you is the Sender and the other is the Receiver. Roles switch every trial.
  • Only the Sender sees the vault code: where both keys must end up and which way they must face.
  • The Sender goes first and can only communicate by moving their own key. Then the Receiver places their key.
  • The vault opens only if both keys end up in the right place, facing the right way.
  • Each turn has a time limit, shown by the bar above the board.
  • Controls: Space makes your key appear, arrow keys move it, A/D rotate it (circles can't rotate), and Space again ends your turn.
  • Don't talk to, gesture to or message your partner. The board is the only way to communicate.
  • Each round you play with a partner on a 3×3 board. One of you is the Sender and the other is the Receiver. Some rounds you will sit out and wait.
  • Only the Sender sees the goal: a tile on the board and the direction the Receiver's triangle must point. The triangle can point 8 ways (up, down, left, right and the 4 diagonals); the small dot marks its tip.
  • The Sender goes first and can only communicate by moving their own key, a circle. Then the Receiver places their triangle and turns it.
  • The round is a success if the Receiver's triangle ends on the goal tile, pointing in the goal direction.
  • Each turn has a time limit, shown by the bar above the board.
  • Your key starts in the middle of the board when your turn begins. Arrow keys move it, A/D rotate the triangle, and Space ends your turn.
  • Don't talk to, gesture to or message anyone. The board is the only way to communicate.
